\section*{Major Comments}

\subsection*{Comment 1: Identification under staggered timing}

\textit{``With treatment timing varying across units, the standard
two-way fixed effects estimator may be biased
(Goodman-Bacon, 2021). The authors should report a heterogeneity-robust
estimator and discuss whether their conclusions hold.''}

\medskip
\noindent We agree, and we now report Callaway-Sant'Anna (2021) as our
preferred estimator (\Cref{tab:main}, column~(3)). Two-way fixed effects
is reported as a benchmark in column~(1). The two estimators agree to
within [X]\%, and Sun-Abraham (2021) yields a similar magnitude. We have
added a paragraph to \Cref{sec:strategy} discussing the choice and a new
\Cref{tab:did_estimators} comparing TWFE, Callaway-Sant'Anna,
Sun-Abraham, and Borusyak-Jaravel-Spiess.

\subsection*{Comment 2: Inference with few clusters}

\textit{``With only [G] treated clusters, conventional cluster-robust
standard errors are likely to under-cover. The authors should report
wild cluster bootstrap p-values.''}

\medskip
\noindent We have added wild cluster bootstrap p-values, computed using
\texttt{boottest} (\Cref{tab:wild_boot}). Bootstrap p-values agree with
conventional inference for the headline coefficient, although they are
moderately wider for the heterogeneity estimates in \Cref{tab:hetero}.
